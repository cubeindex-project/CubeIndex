# Cube Submission Guide

## Introduction

Thank you for contributing to the CubeIndex database.

This guide explains how to submit a cube using the [in-app submission form](/submit/cube).  
Before submitting, please review the requirements below to make sure your entry is complete, accurate, and eligible for inclusion.

## Eligibility requirements

Not every puzzle is eligible for the CubeIndex database. Before submitting a product, confirm that it meets all of the following requirements.

A valid entry must:

- Be a combination puzzle[^combination-puzzle-definition].
- Have been commercially released by an identifiable manufacturer.
- Have been available for purchase to the general public.
- Have been mass-produced.
- Have been sold at physical or online stores.

The following products are generally not eligible:

- Personal modifications.
- Handmade one-off puzzles.
- Privately commissioned puzzles.
- Unreleased prototypes.
- Concept products that were announced but never sold.

Commercially sold third-party modifications may be eligible when they were released as distinct products and can be verified through reliable sources.

When submitting a commercially modified puzzle, use the modifying company as the brand.

Discontinued products remain eligible if they previously met the requirements above.

If you are uncertain whether a product qualifies, contact the CubeIndex team through the [Discord server](/discord) before submitting it.

## Duplicate and version policy

Before creating a submission, search the database for the product name, series, and known alternative names.

Do not create a new entry solely because of:

- A packaging redesign.
- A retailer-created product title.
- A minor manufacturing revision that was not sold as a distinct model.
- A different bundle containing the same puzzle.
- Replacement stickers or accessories.
- A user-adjustable configuration.

A separate entry may be appropriate when the manufacturer sells the product as a distinct version, such as:

- A magnetic and non-magnetic version.
- A MagLev and spring version.
- A ball-core and non-ball-core version.
- A smart and non-smart version.
- An officially named limited edition.
- A mechanically distinct regional or retailer-exclusive version.

## Source requirements

Use reliable sources to verify product information.

Preferred sources include:

1. Official manufacturer product pages.
2. Official manufacturer announcements or catalogues
3. Product packaging or instruction manuals.
4. Reputable specialist retailers.
5. Official distributor pages.

Community databases, marketplace listings, social media posts, videos, and forum discussions may be used as supporting evidence, but they should not normally be the only source when a more authoritative source is available.

When sources conflict:

- Prefer the manufacturer’s official specification.
- Check whether the sources describe different versions.
- Use the most specific source for the exact submitted model.
- Do not average conflicting measurements.
- Report the conflict to the CubeIndex team when it cannot be resolved.

Do not estimate missing information from photographs or similar products.

Unknown optional values should be left blank.

## Field definitions

The following definitions explain the fields used in the submission form.

### Name

The complete official product name used by the manufacturer.

Use the manufacturer’s capitalization where possible.

### Cube version

The cube version describes how the submitted product differs from the standard model.

Available values may include:

- Base: The standard or default version of the cube.
- Variant: An officially sold version with relatively minor changes from the base model, such as a different surface finish, magnet configuration, or tensioning system.
- Limited Edition: A version produced in limited quantities or officially described by the manufacturer as a limited edition.

A product should be entered as a separate version only when it was sold by the manufacturer or an eligible third party as a distinct product configuration.

User-adjustable settings do not create separate versions.

### Series

The series is the manufacturer’s named product family shared by multiple distinct models.

A series should apply across different models, puzzle sizes, or generations rather than merely describing versions of one product.

For example, several different models may belong to the same manufacturer-defined series.

If the correct series is not available, select `Add a new series`. The series selector will be replaced by a field where you can enter the series name. You can use `Back` to return to the existing series list.

Before creating a new series:

- Use the manufacturer’s official name and capitalization.
- Confirm that the series is not already listed.
- Do not use a cube version, feature, or retailer-created product group as a series.

The entered name will be used to create a new series option, so check it carefully before submitting.

### Brand

The company that released the cube, such as GAN, MoYu, or QiYi.

Use the brand shown on the puzzle, packaging, or official product listing.

If the correct brand is not available in the dropdown menu, select `+ Add Brand` at the bottom of the list. An additional field will appear where you can enter the brand name.

Before creating a new brand:

- Use the brand’s official name and capitalization.
- Do not include retailer names, slogans, or additional descriptions.

The entered name will be used to create a new brand option, so check it carefully before submitting.

### Cube type

The main puzzle category to which the cube belongs.

This may be a WCA-recognized event puzzle or a non-WCA puzzle type.

If the correct type is not available in the dropdown menu, select `+ Create Type` at the bottom of the list. An additional field will appear where you can enter the type name.

Before creating a new type:

- Check for alternative names or spellings.
- Confirm that an equivalent type is not already listed.
- Use a recognized and broadly applicable category name.
- Do not use a product name, brand, series, version, or marketing term as a cube type.

The entered name will be used to create a new type option, so check it carefully before submitting.

### Sub-type

The broader family or structure of the puzzle.

For example, a 3×3x3, 4×4x4, and 5×5x5 may all belong to the NxNxN family.

### Related model

The related model is the existing product on which the submitted version, edition, or modification is based.

> [!NOTE]
> This field is only available when the cube is set as modded, as a variant or as a limited edition

### Release date

The date on which the exact product version first became publicly available for purchase.

Use the following format: `YYYY-MM-DD`

Use the first confirmed sale date, not:

- The announcement date.
- The date of a teaser.
- The date on which a retailer later added the product.

### Image URL

A direct URL to a clear image of the exact puzzle version being submitted.

Images with a plain or uncluttered background are preferred. When possible, provide an image URL from the original product page or retailer page. This helps our system identify the source and assign the appropriate credit.

Do not submit links to search-result pages, temporary image previews, or private files.

### Surface finish

Select the factory-applied texture or coating on the puzzle’s exterior.

### Weight

The assembled puzzle’s weight in grams.

Exclude:

- Packaging.
- Display boxes.

Include batteries only when they are installed and required for normal use.

Prefer the manufacturer’s published weight. When no official weight is available, use a measurement from a reputable retailer or trusted reviewer and provide the source when possible.

> [!warning]
> Do not estimate the weight.

### Size

The dimensions of the cube in millimeters.

Use the following format: length x width x height

Example for an equally sized cubic puzzle: 56 x 56 x 56

For non-cubic puzzles, enter the maximum overall dimensions.

Include fixed protrusions that are part of the puzzle’s normal shape. Do not include packaging, stands, keychains, or removable accessories.

> [!warning]
> Do not estimate dimensions.

### Discontinued

- **True:** The manufacturer has ended production or officially retired the product.
- **False:** The product remains in active production or in the manufacturer’s current product range.

Remaining retailer stock does not mean that a product is still being produced.

### Features

#### WCA legal

- **True:** The cube may be used in an official WCA competition under the current regulations[^current-wca-regulation].
- **False:** The cube is not permitted in official WCA competition.

WCA legality may change when regulations are updated.

#### Magnetic

- **True:** The cube contains magnets that assist with alignment or stability.
- **False:** The cube does not contain magnets.

#### Smart

- **True:** The cube contains electronic components, such as Bluetooth connectivity, sensors, or a built-in tracking system.
- **False:** The cube does not contain electronic components.

Decorative lights alone do not necessarily make a puzzle smart unless they are part of an interactive electronic system.

> [!NOTE]
> A smart cube cannot be marked as WCA legal.

#### Modded

- **True:** The product was modified by a third party and commercially sold as a distinct version.
- **False:** The cube is an official, unmodified product released by an eligible manufacturer.

Personal modifications are not eligible for separate database entries.

#### MagLev

- **True:** The cube uses a magnetic levitation tensioning or compression system.
- **False:** The cube uses another tensioning system, such as springs.

#### Ball core

- **True:** The cube contains a ball-shaped magnetic core.
- **False:** The cube does not contain a ball-shaped magnetic core.

#### Stickered

- **True:** The cube was manufactured with removable stickers, whether pre-applied or supplied separately.
- **False:** The cube uses coloured plastic, tiles, printing, or another non-stickered color system.

### Submitter note

Use the submitter note to help reviewers verify the submission. Include links to the official manufacturer page, product announcement, catalogue, reputable retailer listing, or other reliable sources used to complete the form.

Also explain any details that may not be obvious from the form, such as:

- Conflicting information between sources.
- Uncertain release dates, measurements, or features.
- How a variant, limited edition, or modified product differs from a related model.
- Any other unusual detail a reviewer should know.

Write a short, focused note. Do not repeat information that is already clear from the form.

## Vendor links

Vendor links identify stores where the exact cube version can be purchased. They allow CubeIndex to display current pricing and availability information.

Select `Add Vendor` to add a store listing. Add no more than one link for each vendor.

For each listing:

- **Vendor Name:** Select the store selling the product. If the vendor is not listed, contact the CubeIndex team.
- **URL:** Enter the direct product-page URL for the exact cube version being submitted. Do not use a store homepage, search-results page, shortened URL, or a link to a different version, bundle, or product.
- **Price:** Enter the product’s currently displayed price as a number. Use the currency shown beside the field; do not convert the price into another currency.
- **Available:** Select this when the product is currently available to purchase. Leave it unselected when the product is out of stock, discontinued, or otherwise unavailable.

Provide the current price and availability shown on the vendor’s product page at the time of submission. After the submission is approved, an automated script will regularly check the vendor page and keep this information up to date.

## Autofill service

To make submissions easier, CubeIndex provides an autofill service.

When you enter a supported product-page URL, the service attempts to retrieve and complete as many fields as possible. Autofilled information should still be reviewed carefully before submission.

Support for a store does not guarantee that every product page can be processed successfully.

The autofill service may occasionally display a message stating that it is warming up. This is caused by a limitation of the underlying hosting service. If this takes unusually long, reload the page and try again.

If the problem continues, submit a bug report through the CubeIndex [report page](/report) or contact the team through the [Discord server](/discord).

## Submission & reviewing

After you submit a cube, the CubeIndex team will review the entry as soon as possible.

Please check that all information is accurate before submitting. Incomplete or incorrect entries may take longer to review.

If your submission is still marked as Under Review after 48 hours, do not hesitate to contact us.

A submission can receive one of the following outcomes:

- **Approved:** The cube is added to the public database, and your contribution is credited.
- **Refused:** The submission contains an issue or does not meet the database requirements. A staff note will usually explain the reason and any corrections that may be needed.

## Help and Bug reporting

For questions about the submission process, join the [CubeIndex Discord server](/discord). The staff team can help clarify the requirements.

Technical problems should be reported through the CubeIndex [report page](/report).
