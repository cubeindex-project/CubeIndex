# Vendor Submission Guide

## Introduction

Thank you for contributing to the CubeIndex database.

Use the [vendor submission form](/submit/vendor) to suggest a cube shop that is missing from CubeIndex.

Before submitting, search the [vendor catalog](/explore/vendors) to help prevent duplicate submissions.

## Eligibility requirements

A vendor is generally eligible when it:

- Sells twisty puzzles, replacement parts, timers, lubricants, accessories, or other products closely related to cubing.
- Operates an active public online storefront.
- Accepts orders from customers.
- Provides enough official information to identify the business and its location.
- Represents a distinct vendor rather than another version of an existing listing.

A vendor may serve customers locally, nationally, or internationally. International shipping is not required.

The following are normally not accepted:

- **Marketplace-only sellers:** Sellers operating only through platforms such as general online marketplaces.
- **Social-media-only sellers:** Sellers that accept orders only through social media, messaging apps, or private messages.
- **Inactive stores:** Websites that no longer accept orders, have closed, or appear abandoned.
- **Temporary listings:** Short-term event shops, limited private sales, or temporary storefronts.
- **Private sellers:** Individuals selling privately without a stable public storefront.
- **Duplicate regional URLs:** Country, language, or regional versions of an existing vendor’s website when they represent the same business.

## Source requirements

Use official, first-party sources whenever possible.

The vendor’s official website should be the primary source for:

- Vendor name.
- Homepage URL.
- Business location.
- Default currency.
- Logo.

Official social-media pages may be used as supporting evidence, but they should not replace the vendor’s official store website.

## Field definitions

### Vendor name

Enter the vendor’s official store or trading name.

Preserve the capitalization and styling normally used by the vendor, while keeping the entry readable.

Do not add:

- Advertising slogans.
- Unofficial abbreviations.
- Country or region names that are not part of the official name.
- Legal suffixes unless the vendor normally uses them publicly.

:white_check_mark: Example Cubes  
:x: Example Cubes – Best Speedcubes in Canada

### Homepage URL

Enter the official homepage of the vendor’s online store.

The URL must:

- Begin with https:// or http://.
- Lead to the vendor’s main storefront.
- Be publicly accessible.

Do not submit:

- A shortened URL.
- A marketplace listing.
- A social-media profile.

### Country code

Enter the two-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code[^iso-3166-2-list] for the country where the vendor is based.

Examples:

- US - United States
- GB - United Kingdom
- CA - Canada
- JP - Japan
- AU - Australia

Country codes are stored in uppercase.

Use the vendor’s business location, not:

- Your own location.
- The location of its web server.
- A country to which the vendor ships.
- A warehouse location that does not represent the business itself.

When a vendor operates from multiple countries, use the country associated with its main business or primary storefront.

### Currency

Select the vendor’s primary currency using the corresponding three-letter ISO 4217 currency code[^iso-4217-list].

When the store supports only one currency, select that currency.

When the store supports multiple currencies, select the currency that:

1. Is shown by default to customers in the vendor’s home country; or
2. Appears to be the store’s primary operating currency.

Do not select a currency only because it is the one shown to you after automatic location detection.

For example, a store based in Japan may display US dollars to an international visitor while using Japanese yen as its primary domestic currency. In that case, select JPY.

### Vendor logo

Use the vendor’s official logo rather than a product image, banner, photograph, screenshot, or unofficial recreation.

File requirements

- Accepted formats: JPEG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF.
- Maximum file size: 512 KB.
- Use a clear, high-quality image.
- Frame the logo closely without cutting off any part of the artwork.
- Remove unrelated text, graphics, borders, or background elements.
- Transparent or plain backgrounds generally produce the best result.

CubeIndex converts uploaded logos into a transparent 512 × 512 WebP image. The artwork is not cropped during conversion, so the uploaded file should already have appropriate framing and spacing.

Before uploading, check that:

- The complete logo is visible.
- Small text remains readable.
- The image is not blurry or heavily compressed.
- There is not excessive empty space around the logo.
- The file does not contain unrelated promotional artwork.

## Submission and review

After submission, the vendor remains private while it is pending review.

You can follow the result from [your submissions page](/user/submissions). Rejected submissions include a staff note explaining what needs correction.
