# CubeIndex Submission Manual

## Introduction

Thank you for contributing to the CubeIndex database. All catalog submissions are reviewed by the CubeIndex team before they become public.

Choose the guide for the item you want to contribute:

- [Submit a cube](/docs/submission-manual/cubes)
- [Submit a vendor](/docs/submission-manual/vendors)

## Review process

Please check that all information is accurate before submitting. Incomplete or incorrect entries may take longer to review.

A submission can receive one of the following outcomes:

- **Pending:** The submission is waiting for staff review.
- **Approved:** The entry is added to the public database.
- **Rejected:** The submission contains an issue or does not meet the database requirements. A staff note will normally explain the reason.

If a submission remains pending after 48 hours, contact the team through the [Discord server](/discord).

## Help and bug reporting

For questions about eligibility or fields, join the [CubeIndex Discord server](/discord).

Technical problems should be reported through the CubeIndex [report page](/report).
